Our Fundraising Program

Ready to make your fundraising efforts a success? Fill out the form today to learn more about our car wash fundraising options and schedule your event. Together, we’ll drive your cause forward!

  • Why Fundraise with Us?

    • Increased Awareness & Visibility – Gain exposure for your organization while making a difference.
    • Eco-Friendly Approach – Our car washes use significantly less water than traditional parking lot fundraisers.
    • Guaranteed Donation – We contribute $1 per car washed or $500, whichever is greater.
    • Extra Support – Earn an additional $5 for each new member sign-up during your event!
    • Professional Results – Participants get high-quality washes while supporting a great cause!

  • How It Works

    1. Submit Your Application – Fill out our Fundraiser Agreement and provide your organization’s details.
    2. Plan Your Event – Choose a date and spread the word to maximize participation.
    3. Raise Funds – Every car washed means more money for your organization!
    4. Get Paid – We’ll process your donation and send the funds directly to your cause.

  • Get Started Today!

    Complete the Fundraising Application and email your 501(c)(3) certificate or Nonprofit Determination Letter to info@freshfenders.com.
